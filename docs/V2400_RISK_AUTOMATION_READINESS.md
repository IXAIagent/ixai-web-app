# V24.00 Risk Automation Readiness

## Goal

V24 models risk automation readiness without scheduling, sending, or trading.

## Scope

- Risk rule model.
- Risk trigger model.
- Alert evaluation readiness.
- Snapshot comparison model.
- Automation diagnostics.

## Boundaries

- No scheduler or cron.
- No Telegram, email, LINE, push, or notification sender.
- No trading action, order execution, buy/sell/rebalance instruction, or recommendation logic.
- No database write, SQL, migration, broker, AI provider, or external fetch.
