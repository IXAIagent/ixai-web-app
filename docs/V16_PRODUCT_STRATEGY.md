# V16 Product Strategy

V16 is a product strategy upgrade, not a feature sprint.

IXAI now has two complementary product lines:

```text
Product A — AI Financial Media
Product B — AI Investment Monitoring
```

The strategic goal is to make IXAI both:

- A daily AI financial media habit for public visitors.
- A personal AI investment monitoring system for Workspace users.
- An always-on AI layer that works before the user opens the app.

Permanent product philosophy:

```text
The value of IXAI is not information.
The value of IXAI is relevance.
```

Source of truth:

- `docs/IXAI_PRODUCT_PHILOSOPHY.md`

## 1. Strategic Positioning

IXAI is not only a Workspace and not only a Brief product.

IXAI consists of two complementary products:

Product A:

```text
AI Financial Media
Helping everyone understand what matters in today's market.
```

Product B:

```text
AI Investment Monitoring
Helping every investor understand what matters to their own investments.
```

These products share the same brand trust layer, but they answer different questions:

| Product | Surface | Core Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Financial Media | Daily Brief / Weekly Brief | 今天市場發生什麼？ |
| AI Investment Monitoring | Workspace | 今天哪些事情影響我的投資？ |

## 2. Product A — AI Financial Media

- Weekly Brief

```text
每天讓使用者有理由回到 IXAI。
```

```text

```

Weekly Brief answers:


```text
每天五分鐘。
```

- 熱榜

But Product A must not become another high-volume news list.

Public Brief is:


The value is curation, ranking, and narrative clarity, not article count.

## 4. Product Funnel

The IXAI product funnel is:

```text
Public Visitor
↓
Daily Brief
↓
Weekly Brief
↓
建立信任
↓
建立使用習慣
↓
建立 Workspace
↓
輸入資產
↓
AI Monitoring
↓
Subscription
```

Daily / Weekly should make the public visitor come back.

Workspace should make the signed-in user feel that IXAI is watching the things that matter to their own portfolio.

SaaS should not be rushed at this stage.

The short-term goal is to open the main experience to users, find the Wow Moment, and only then plan paid tiers.

Wow Moment:

```text
原來 AI 可以每天幫我省下看盤、看新聞、追 FCN 的時間。
```

Subscription becomes appropriate after:

- Users return daily.
- Workspace delivers personalized monitoring value.
- Telegram / AI Monitoring creates retention.
- FCN monitoring becomes meaningfully hard to replace.

## 5. Product Principles

1. Daily / Weekly are not a free Workspace.
   - They are AI Financial Media.

2. Workspace is not a news reader.
   - It is AI Investment Monitoring.

3. Public answers:
   - 今天市場發生什麼？

4. Workspace answers:
   - 今天哪些事情影響我的投資？

5. Daily / Weekly cannot depend on the user's Portfolio.
   - They must remain public, editorial, and generally useful.

6. Workspace must depend on the user's Portfolio.
   - Without the user's assets, it cannot be true investment monitoring.

7. IXAI does not give investment advice.
   - It monitors, explains, prioritizes, and reminds.

## 7. Workspace Principles

Workspace is not:

- Market Intelligence
- News homepage
- Chat shell
- Engineering dashboard

Workspace is:

- My Intelligence
- Portfolio-aware monitoring
- FCN-aware monitoring
- Watchlist-aware monitoring
- Risk-aware daily focus

Workspace Home is not a news homepage.

Workspace Home is:

```text
Today Focus
```

Today Focus answers:

```text
今天哪三件事情真正影響我的投資？
```

Every Workspace item should explain:

- Which asset is affected.
- Which portfolio or FCN is affected.
- Why it matters.
- What the user should monitor next.

Workspace should not wait until the user opens the app to start analysis.

Always-on monitoring flow:

```text
News
↓
Events
↓
Prices
↓
Earnings
↓
Portfolio
↓
FCN
↓
Crypto
↓
Risk
↓
AI Analysis
↓
Today Focus
↓
Telegram / Workspace
```

When the user logs in, Workspace should show the work AI has already completed.

V16B keeps the existing nine engine roadmap:

1. Global Asset Intelligence
2. News Relevance
3. Event Intelligence
4. FCN Intelligence
5. Portfolio Intelligence
6. AI Monitoring Feed
7. Today Focus
8. Copilot AI Analyst
9. Memory Engine

V16B remains the Workspace intelligence roadmap.

It must answer:

```text
這件事為什麼跟我的投資有關？
```

### Telegram-first Notification Strategy

V16 should not rush into full App Push.

Telegram is the first external notification channel because it already matches the user's monitoring workflow.

Examples:

- FCN observation tomorrow.
- FCN KI distance approaching.
- Underlying earnings tonight.
- TSLA large move affects 3 FCNs.
- BTC volatility rising.
- Today Focus Top 3 ready.

Every notification must answer:

```text
Why does this matter to my investments?
這件事為什麼跟我的投資有關？
```

## 11. Success Metrics

AI Financial Media success is not news quantity.

Success is:

```text
每天都值得閱讀。
```

Signals:

- Daily return habit.
- Weekly completion.
- Share intent.
- Clear narrative recall.
- Lower repetition.
- Higher trust.

Workspace success is not page quantity.

Success is:

```text
AI 真正替使用者監控投資。
```

Signals:

- User can understand today’s top 3 investment-impact items.
- News is connected to actual assets.
- FCN risk is visible without manual digging.
- Portfolio impact is explained.
- Copilot answers are grounded in user holdings.

## 12. Compliance Boundary

Both product lines remain:

- Monitoring
- Awareness
- Intelligence
- Workflow
- Risk explanation

Both product lines are not:

- Broker
- Trading system
- Signal seller
- Robo-advisor
- Discretionary manager
- Unlicensed investment advisory service

Forbidden product language:

- 買進
- 賣出
- 保證
- 必漲
- 必跌
- 自動下單
- 我建議你買

Allowed product language:

- 需要留意
- 風險上升
- 受到影響
- 建議查看
- 下一個觀察日
- 目前資料顯示
- 這不是投資建議
