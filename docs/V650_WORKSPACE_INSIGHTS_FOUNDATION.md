# V6.50 Workspace Insights Foundation

## Goal

Create deterministic insight cards on top of Workspace Graph.

## Behavior

Insights summarize graph health, risk signals, FCN risk, FCN schedule, watchlist quote gaps, and system stability. They explain what needs attention; they do not tell users what to trade.

## Boundary

No AI model calls, investment recommendations, buy/sell/hold language, broker actions, or order execution.
